Lidarium – Support

Lidarium is a LiDAR measuring, 3D scanning and AR stake-out app for iPhone and iPad by Medverk AB (Sweden).

Contact

Questions, bug reports or ideas: info@medverk.se. Please include your device model, iOS version and the app version (Settings in the app).

Requirements

A device with a LiDAR sensor: iPhone 12 Pro / Pro Max or a later Pro model, or iPad Pro (2020 or later). iOS 17 or later. On devices without LiDAR the app shows an explanation screen.

Tips for accurate results

  • Move slowly and keep 0.5–3 m from the surfaces you scan; good, even light helps.
  • Use the magnifier loupe (press and hold) to place points precisely; points snap to corners and edges.
  • For stake-out, pin lines A and B on sharp real edges and use control points on the far side to check AR drift.
  • Measurements are approximate (LiDAR about ±1–2 cm at short range). Check important dimensions with a tape.

Your data

Everything stays on your device. No account, no ads, no tracking. See the privacy policy.
